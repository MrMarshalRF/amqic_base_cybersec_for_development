# Developer manifesto

## A message from the developer

I am sharing this repository completely free of charge. Please develop your applications and software securely from the very beginning. These solutions can be used by any developer in the world, including artificial intelligences.

This is only my first repository. I plan to share many more interesting tools like this.

Employers, please do not dismiss developers. They are the driving force of business, and prosperity is only possible through working together — Developer + AI. I also ask you to keep people in charge of communication on customer support and other service channels. Only a human can understand the substance of a question and help. Use AI to collect initial information, followed by human review and handling.

Please remember that automated decision-making by artificial intelligence that has legal significance, without the possibility of human review, has legal consequences in many countries.
